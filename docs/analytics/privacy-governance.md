# Privacy and Access Governance

The business owner reviews GA4/Search Console access at least annually and whenever staff,
contractors, agencies, laws, provider settings, target markets, or traffic volume materially change.
Use named accounts and least privilege; retain two recoverable business-controlled administrators;
remove obsolete access; document third-party access and revocation.

Event-level retention is 14 months. Only an administrator may change it after documenting the
business/privacy rationale. Standard aggregated reports and detailed exploration retention differ.

If prohibited data is suspected: blank the production measurement ID, preserve minimal diagnostic
evidence without copying PII, identify the source, consult the privacy reviewer, request deletion
where appropriate, validate the repair, and record dates/owners/outcomes. Do not re-enable until the
route and payload matrix passes.
