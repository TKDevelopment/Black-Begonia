# Access and Ownership Governance

The florist/business owner is the accountable property owner. Maintain at least two recoverable
business-controlled administrators. Everyone else uses a named account and the lowest role needed:
report viewers do not receive configuration access.

Review access annually and whenever staff, agencies, contractors, or ownership changes. Record the
reviewer/date, test administrator recovery, remove obsolete users, time-limit third-party access,
and document the reason, approver, expiration, transfer, and revocation. Never share passwords or
commit credentials/recovery information.
