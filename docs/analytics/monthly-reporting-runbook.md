# Monthly Search and Analytics Runbook

Use Search Console for pre-click queries, impressions, clicks, CTR, average position, landing pages,
devices, broad geography, and changes over time. Expect reporting delay and anonymized-query gaps.

Use GA4 for eligible post-click acquisition, approved landing-page engagement, inquiry starts,
confirmed inquiries, device category, campaigns, and content contribution. Compare—but do not force
reconciliation between—the two products.

Monthly questions:

- Which service/location pages gained qualified Google visibility?
- Which landing pages contributed to confirmed inquiries?
- Which sources and consistently named campaigns produced leads?
- Where do mobile and desktop visitors abandon the inquiry journey?
- Which pages have visibility without engagement, or engagement without visibility?

Label delayed, thresholded, consent-limited, blocked, or low-volume results. Avoid causal claims and
major decisions from small samples. Build the first baseline over 60–90 days, then annotate material
content, CTA, form, campaign, partnership, performance, and technical SEO changes.

## Baseline follow-up

Owner: florist/business property owner. Activation date and the resulting 60–90-day due window must
be entered in the validation record at activation. Native report locations: Search Console
Performance and GA4 Acquisition, Landing Page, Pages and Screens, Events, and Explorations. Treat
low-volume comparisons as directional and do not claim causation.
