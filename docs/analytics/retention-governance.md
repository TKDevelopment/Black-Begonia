# Retention Governance

Approved default: 14 months of event-level exploration data with reset-on-new-activity disabled.
The business property administrator owns this setting and reviews it annually with the privacy
configuration. Detailed exploration retention differs from aggregated standard reporting.

A change requires a documented reporting need, privacy review, owner, effective date, and assessment
of whether an external long-term export is justified. No warehouse is part of this release.
