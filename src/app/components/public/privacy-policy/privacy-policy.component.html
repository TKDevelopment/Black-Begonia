Privacy Policy

Black Begonia Floral Designs, LLC uses the information you choose to provide to respond to inquiries, provide floral services, and operate this website. This policy also explains the limited analytics used on approved public website pages.

Information you provide

Inquiry forms may request contact details, event information, preferences, and inspiration links. We use that information to evaluate and respond to your request. Inquiry records are business records in our customer relationship system; they are not sent to Google Analytics.

Public website analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 on approved public marketing pages to understand how visitors find the website, which content is useful, and whether public journeys contribute to a successfully submitted inquiry. Depending on your region and choices, this may use first-party cookies or similar browser storage.

Measurement may include a sanitized public page category, general acquisition source, campaign labels, device category, broad geography provided by Google, selected public calls to action, portfolio or service engagement, inquiry starts, and confirmation that an inquiry was accepted. Analytics can be incomplete because of consent choices, browser protections, unavailable scripts, or ad blockers.

We intentionally exclude names, email addresses, phone numbers, mailing addresses, inquiry answers, free text, event dates tied to a person, customer or project identifiers, payment details, authentication data, secure tokens, and complete personalized URLs.

Your analytics choice

For visitors confidently identified as being in the United States, privacy-restricted analytics may be enabled by default with a clear opt-out. For visitors outside the United States, or when location cannot be determined, analytics remains off until it is affirmatively enabled. Global Privacy Control is honored by disabling analytics. You may use the Analytics Preferences control in the website footer to enable, decline, or withdraw analytics at any time. Declining does not prevent browsing or submitting an inquiry.

We do not use cookieless Google Analytics pings after an opt-out. Advertising storage, Google Signals, personalized advertising, remarketing, audience activation, URL passthrough, and Google Ads integration are disabled.

Pages never measured

Google Analytics does not load on CRM and administrator pages, secure payment pages, login pages, password recovery or reset pages, local development, or unapproved preview and staging environments.

Providers, retention, and access

Google acts as our website analytics provider. GA4 event-level exploration data is retained for 14 months. Access is limited to named people who need it for business reporting or configuration, and access is reviewed periodically. Aggregated reports may remain available under Google's reporting rules after event-level data expires.

Questions and updates

This policy and the analytics configuration are reviewed at least annually and when laws, Google settings, target markets, or traffic volume materially change. For privacy questions, use the contact information published on this website. This policy describes our practices and is not a promise that analytics captures every visitor or interaction.
